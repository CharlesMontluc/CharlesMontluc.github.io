About

I'm in the third year of AIDAMS, the joint bachelor in Artificial Intelligence, Data and Management Sciences run by CentraleSupélec and ESSEC. The programme sits between mathematics, computer science and finance, and that is where I like to work: taking a real problem from a business, and building the data or AI system that solves it.

So far that has meant a retrieval system over aircraft maintenance manuals at Safran, automation pipelines at Société Générale, and side projects from a planning bot that learns how long my tasks actually take to a tool that transliterates song lyrics across alphabets.

I grew up between French and Greek, which is probably why languages and scripts keep showing up in what I build. Away from the keyboard, I play chess and table tennis.
